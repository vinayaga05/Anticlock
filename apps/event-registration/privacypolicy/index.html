This Privacy Policy explains how MOMSMERCY ("we", "us" or "our") handles personal information when you use the Knock application, its related Anticlock services, and websites that link to this policy (together, the "Platform"). It explains what information we process, why we use it, who can receive it, and the choices available to you. Our registered address is [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

This policy covers customers, visitors, creators, business owners, service providers and other users. An independent provider, seller or external website may also handle information under its own privacy notice. We remain responsible for our own processing and the responsibilities that applicable law places on us.

The separate event-registration service at knock.org.in, including its registration roster, requires an event-specific privacy notice and is outside this policy unless that notice expressly adopts it and explains registration-data visibility. Do not assume that a registration website has the same privacy settings as the mobile app.

Information you give us

Account and profile information includes your mobile number, name, profile photograph, biography, website, location, account preferences and other details you choose to add. We process authentication requests and session information to sign you in and protect access to your account.

Service and transaction information includes searches and enquiries, selected providers, service descriptions, appointment times, booking history, delivery or service addresses, order details, amounts, payment status, transaction references, support requests and dispute information. If you submit payment proof for a linked event or service, it can reveal your name, bank or UPI reference and transaction details. Conceal unrelated balances and transactions before uploading it. Do not give us your UPI PIN, banking password, card security code or account login OTP.

Business and provider information includes business contacts, address and service area, categories, prices, availability, qualifications, certifications, experience, application details and documents submitted for review. Identity or business documents may be requested where necessary for verification or a legal requirement. These documents are handled separately from public listing content. Submit only requested information and use a masked identity document where that is accepted.

Content and communications include Clips, Flash posts, Stories, photographs, videos, captions, comments, reviews, likes, follows, saves, event submissions, community complaints, messages, attachments and information you send to support. Communication features also process participant identifiers, timestamps, conversation or call context and delivery information.

Fitness and sensitive information

Fitness is optional. If you use it, we process the assessment and records you provide, such as date of birth, height, weight, goals, activity, food preferences, workout and meal records, water, sleep, steps, habits, measurements and progress media. Optional health fields can include allergies, injuries, mobility restrictions, medical conditions, medicines, pregnancy or breastfeeding information and eating disorder concerns. You may leave optional fields blank.

We use this information to maintain your Fitness space, calculate wellness estimates, prepare and display plans, record progress and provide the expert services you request. Health information and private progress media are not intended for public profiles, search, Clips or general community feeds. Choosing to publish a health detail or image in a public post makes that particular content visible to its audience.

An eligible trainer or nutritionist receives access through the Fitness sharing process only to the categories you authorise, subject to the booking or client relationship and access controls. You can revoke that access. Revocation stops future access through that permission; it cannot recall a copy already lawfully received or remove a professional's independent legal recordkeeping duties. Contact us if you believe access is inappropriate.

Information collected through use

We process technical and activity information needed to operate and protect the Platform, including IP address, device or installation identifiers, operating system, application version, request times, errors, session activity, interactions with features and notification tokens. Some security controls use hashed phone, device or network identifiers. A hash can still be personal information when it can be linked to you.

When enabled, location helps find nearby businesses, calculate distance and select a service area or address. You can enter an area manually instead of granting device location access where the feature supports it. We do not need continuous background location to provide nearby discovery.

We may receive relevant information from a provider fulfilling your booking, another user mentioning or reporting your account, a communications or payment service, and verification sources you authorise or that we may lawfully use. We do not obtain permission to use unrelated personal information merely because another user supplied it.

Device permissions and local storage

Camera and photo access allow you to choose or capture a profile picture, content, verification material or private Fitness media. Microphone access supports video recording, voice input and calls. Speech recognition may use your device platform's speech service. Location access supports nearby discovery and maps. Notification permission allows booking, message, security and other enabled alerts.

You can change these permissions in device settings. Refusing or withdrawing a permission can prevent the associated feature from working, while other available features remain usable. Login uses the code you enter; we do not require access to your entire SMS inbox or address book for that purpose.

The app uses local storage for sessions, preferences and saved state. Websites and embedded services may use cookies or similar storage for essential functions. Where optional analytics or advertising technologies require consent, they must be disclosed with a separate choice before activation. Browser controls can restrict cookies, although some functions may then fail.

Why we use information

We use relevant information to create and manage accounts; deliver services you request; show listings and content; route enquiries; manage bookings and orders; enable chat and calls; maintain private Fitness records; deliver notifications; handle support and complaints; detect fraud and abuse; troubleshoot errors; and meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We process information with consent where required and on another lawful basis only where applicable law permits it. Accepting our Terms does not by itself authorise every optional use of your information. We ask for a separate choice for sensitive sharing, optional AI meal analysis and other processing that requires specific consent. We do not use private Fitness records or identity documents as public promotional content without your separate permission.

Promotional communications, if offered, require the applicable consent and an accessible way to opt out. Opting out does not stop essential security messages or communications needed for an active booking, order or support request. Providers may use enquiry details to respond to your request; they must not treat an enquiry as permission for unrelated marketing.

What other people can see

Personal profiles are public by default unless an available privacy setting changes their audience. Published content can appear in feeds, search, profiles or shared links according to its audience and moderation status. Your name, avatar and contribution can appear together. Followers-only settings do not prevent an authorised viewer from taking a screenshot or making a copy.

Business profiles can show the business name, logo, published telephone number, email, website, address, map position, services, prices, media, qualifications and reviews. A provider introduction video may become a public Clip after approval. Confirm that you are authorised to publish each business contact and location.

Community complaints are visible to other signed-in users and include the complaint text, your display name, avatar and posting details. They are separate from a confidential support or legal grievance. Do not post identity documents, health records, payment proof, private addresses or another person's contact information in that feed. Send sensitive evidence to the private contact listed below.

Stories normally leave the active feed after 24 hours. Feed expiry does not mean that all stored copies, reports, backups or recipients' copies are erased at that time. Messages and call details are shared with the people involved and the services necessary to deliver them. A recipient can keep or forward information you send.

Service providers and other recipients

We use infrastructure and specialist services to deliver the features you choose. These can include Hostinger for application hosting, Cloudflare R2 for media storage and delivery, Cloudflare Stream for video processing and playback, BulkBlaster for login SMS, Google Maps for maps and place services, Firebase Cloud Messaging and device platform services for notifications, and Stream (GetStream) for configured chat and audio or video calls. A provider receives the information needed for its task, which can include account identifiers, phone numbers, content, location, notification tokens or communication data as relevant.

Payment providers, banks or UPI applications process payments where a payment route is actually offered. A listing, booking request or pending payment label is not proof that money has been collected by us. Information you provide directly to an external payment service is also governed by that service's privacy terms.

We disclose necessary information to the business or seller you contact, booking participants, authorised personnel handling verification or support, and professional advisers under appropriate duties. We may disclose limited information when required by a valid legal process, to meet legal obligations, or where law permits action to address fraud or a serious safety risk. A business transfer may involve information subject to the applicable safeguards and notice requirements.

AI and communications

If you use Genie, your messages and relevant recent conversation context are processed by the configured AI service, which may be Groq or OpenAI. Voice input can also be processed by the applicable speech recognition service. Avoid entering secrets, identity numbers or unnecessary health details. Available assistant controls allow you to manage personalisation and request export or deletion of assistant history.

Optional AI meal analysis asks for confirmation before sending the selected meal image to OpenAI. The analysis is an editable estimate. It does not establish exact nutrition, allergens or medical suitability. AI processing is separate from sharing your Fitness space with a trainer. Provider processing locations and retention depend on the service and the arrangements in effect; this policy does not promise zero retention by an external AI provider.

Call features process audio and video for the requested communication. If a recording or transcription feature is introduced, its purpose and applicable consent must be shown before recording starts. Do not record or redistribute another person's communication without the required permission. We do not describe chats or calls as end-to-end encrypted under this policy.

Retention and account closure

We retain information for the purposes described above and for the periods required by law, including necessary transaction, fraud, security, complaint and dispute records. Different categories need different retention periods. Backups and legally preserved records may outlast active use; retained information must remain protected and restricted to its justified purpose.

You can use the available Fitness controls to export Fitness records or delete Fitness data. An export may contain records and media references rather than every original media file. Download any files you wish to keep separately. Assistant history has its own privacy controls.

The current account closure process deactivates the account, revokes sessions and initiates Fitness data deletion. It does not automatically erase every booking, order, message, public contribution or record held by an external service. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] to request review, correction, export or erasure of remaining personal information. We will explain any information that must be retained and the reason, subject to lawful restrictions. Uninstalling the app does not close your account or erase server records.

Your choices and requests

You can update available profile fields and privacy settings, change device permissions, manage notification preferences, withdraw optional consent and revoke Fitness sharing. If withdrawing information prevents a requested feature from operating, we will explain the effect. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] to seek access, correction, a copy, deletion, consent withdrawal assistance or a privacy grievance. We may verify that the request comes from you, using information proportionate to the request. Please do not email an unmasked identity document unless a secure and necessary verification process has been explained. Rights and exceptions depend on the law in force; this policy does not restrict statutory rights. Where an applicable nomination or representative process is available, you may request assistance through the same contact.

Security and international processing

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including protected authentication and additional controls for private Fitness data and media. No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and account secure and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Some technology providers may process information outside India. Transfers must meet applicable Indian requirements and the protections required for the information concerned. We do not claim that all Platform data stays in India. Where an incident triggers a legal notice or reporting duty, we will follow the applicable requirements.

Age and changes

Knock is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not offered as a child-directed service. If you believe a child has provided information without an authorised, lawful process, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. An adult must not create a child account by accepting these terms on the child's behalf without an expressly supported process.

We will publish material changes with an updated effective date and provide additional notice or obtain fresh consent where required. New processing that requires consent will not be authorised merely by changing this policy.
